Website privacy

This notice explains the information handled by the Enterprise Message Agents marketing website at imessageagents.org. Customer deployments and their end-user conversations are governed by the arrangements agreed for each deployment.

Browsing this website

Cloudflare hosts and protects this site. Requests pass through Cloudflare, which processes technical request information to deliver and secure the service. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its practices.

Demo requests

Our booking links take you to Google Calendar. Information you choose to submit there is shared with the appointment organizer to arrange and follow up on your demo. Google handles the booking page under its privacy policy. Do not include sensitive customer information in booking notes.

Interactive example

The John’s Pizza Shop conversation is a fictional, browser-only demonstration. It does not send iMessages or place orders. Text entered in that example stays in the page’s memory and is cleared when the page reloads.

Website measurement

Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregate traffic and performance measurements without advertising cookies. We also measure demo-link clicks by button location to understand which parts of the website are useful. These events contain the event name and button location, not names, email addresses, message text, or user identifiers. A click is not treated as a confirmed booking. Demo-click measurement respects Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals. We do not add advertising cookies or cross-site tracking identifiers.

Contact

For questions about information provided during booking, reply to your appointment confirmation. You can also reach the team through our contact page.
